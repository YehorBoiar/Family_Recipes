#### Omelette with herbs

1. Add eggs to a bowl with some herbs and salt.

   * herbs: chives, parsley, chervil, tarragon
3. Mix the eggs until the texture is consistent.
4. Heat up a small pan and add butter.

   * A small pan makes the omelette easier to roll.
5. Pour the eggs into the pan.
6. Move the eggs around as much as possible to avoid forming a crust.
7. Once cooked:

   * move the egg to one side of the pan
   * roll one half of the omelette
   * hit or toss the pan slightly to help roll the other half
   * finish rolling
8. Carefully place the omelette onto a plate.
