### Savory Pie Recipe

**Dough**
* 300g sour cream
* 4 eggs
* 1 tsp salt
* 1 tsp sugar
* 1 tsp baking powder
* Flour (as needed)

**Filling & Glaze**
* Minced meat
* Mushrooms (any kind)
* Onions
* Rice
* Adjika (or tomato paste mixed with a sauce like Sweet Chili)
* Grated cheese
* Salt & pepper (to taste)
* Garlic, salt, 1 tsp oil, 1 tbsp water (for garlic water glaze)

**Instructions**
1. **Dough:** Mix the sour cream, eggs, salt, sugar, and baking powder. Add enough flour to knead a soft dough. Wrap in plastic wrap and let rest for 1 hour.
2. **Filling:** Fry the minced meat with onions. Separately, fry the mushrooms with onions. Season both with salt and pepper. Boil the rice. 
3. **Assembly:** Grease a baking dish with oil. Roll out the dough and place it into the dish so the edges hang over the sides.
4. **Layering:** Spread the adjika (or tomato paste mixture) on the bottom of the dough. Layer the minced meat, rice, mushrooms, and top with grated cheese.
5. **Bake:** Bake at 180°C for 40 minutes.
6. **Glaze:** Mash the garlic with salt, then mix in the oil and water. Brush this garlic water over the pie immediately after baking.
